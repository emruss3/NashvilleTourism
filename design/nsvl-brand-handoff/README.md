## Current decisions
Paper White #F5F3ED and Charcoal Ink #1F2421. No third accent. Exact NSVL identity. Homepage H1: “Get into Nashville.” Planner headline: “One good plan changes everything.” Mobile-first, with 60% expected mobile traffic. The planner optimizes around occasion, headcount, age ranges, dates, budget and preferences.

This is a design and implementation package, not a working site. Generated photographs/listings are illustrative. Final vector logo, small favicon and standalone image exports remain production dependencies; paper-white reconstructed PNGs are review-only. Implementation instructions must not treat mockups as inventory or completed integrations.
