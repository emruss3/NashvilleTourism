# Direct photo requests

**Do not send outreach yet.** This queue prepares commercial digital editorial permission for exact-place photography on NashRoam.com.

NashRoam is a commercial travel website and may earn advertising, affiliate, and booking revenue. Vague “press use” permission is not enough.

## Reusable request template

Subject: Photo permission request — NashRoam.com (commercial digital editorial use)

Hello,

I’m writing from NashRoam (https://www.nashroam.com), an independent Nashville travel and planning website. We would like permission to use a specific high-resolution photograph of **[BUSINESS NAME]** on NashRoam.com.

**Intended use**

- Commercial digital editorial use on NashRoam.com
- Neighborhood guides, venue / restaurant / hotel guides, and trip-planning pages
- NashRoam may display advertising and may earn affiliate or booking-related revenue on the same site

**Photograph**

- Description: **[DESIRED PHOTO / COMPOSITION]**
- Where we saw a composition we like (reference only — not a license claim): **[SOURCE]**
- Preferred: high-resolution file suitable for web (ideally ≥ 2000px on the long edge)

**Credit**

We will credit the photographer / rights holder as you specify.

**What we need in writing**

Permission for NashRoam to use the photograph for commercial digital editorial use on NashRoam.com, including on a travel website that may contain advertising, affiliate, and booking revenue.

We are happy to use your preferred file, crop respectfully, and link to your official site.

Thank you,
[Name]
NashRoam
hello@nashroam.com

- CVC / Visit Music City URLs in `docs/media/reference/` describe compositions only — never attach them as the licensed file source.

- If permission is declined, move the row to `shoot-instead` and add the subject to `NEEDED-SHOOTS.md`.
